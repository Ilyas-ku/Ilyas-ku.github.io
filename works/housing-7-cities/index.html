Housing in Germany's 7 Largest Cities

New flats completed in 2021–2025, asking rents and flat purchase prices in Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Cologne, Frankfurt, Stuttgart and Düsseldorf. Official and academic sources only, linked below each block.

Completions: new flats in new residential buildings

In 2025 the seven cities together completed 27,711 flats, down from 36,296 a year earlier (−24%). Only Cologne grew. Frankfurt stayed flat. The largest drops were in Stuttgart (−65%), Berlin (−35%) and Munich (−33%).

Rent: asking rents

From 2020 to 2025 asking rents rose everywhere, from +11% in Stuttgart to +23% in Berlin. Munich is the most expensive at €21.01/m² in 2025.

Average asking rent for re-lets in online listings, €/m² excluding utilities. Revised BBSR series (2026). Source: BBSR, Wohnungsmieten. Listings overstate actual new-lease rents: cheap flats are often let without being advertised.

Buying: flat prices

Prices rose to a peak in 2022, fell in 2023 and have recovered slowly since. By 2025 they were above the 2020 level in Cologne (+20%) and Berlin (+16%). Munich is back at its 2020 level.
